Hi, I’m Alex Garber

Regional leader, Orlando

I started selling candy in 4th grade and got in trouble for it two years in a row. In 6th grade I hired a 7th and 8th grader to sell for me. At 18 I started an Instagram clothing brand that sold 3 shirts its first week. I figured out the marketing myself and grew it to 1,000+ customers and $44K before it stalled in college.

That’s when I found door-to-door sales. I made $97,000 my first year. Six years and 1,200+ installs later, I’m the youngest and fastest rep to ever hit Regional in my company, my team won The Cup in 2023, and I’ve trained reps who now outsell me. I bought my first rental property in 2021 and own four houses now.

I had a lot of failed businesses before sales. This is the skill that changed everything, and I’ll teach it to you myself.

These are my results and the results of reps I’ve trained. They aren’t typical and they aren’t a promise.
