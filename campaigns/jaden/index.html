Hi, I’m Jaden Ponto

Your team leader & founder of REV Strategic Equities

I grew up in a small town in northern Minnesota. My dad was in and out of jail, and my mom raised me paycheck to paycheck. At 13 I was driving an hour each way to work at a truck wash. In high school I had a 4.0, played football on Friday nights, and worked 14-hour weekend shifts to help pay the mortgage.

I played college football at St. John’s until COVID killed the season. One entrepreneur I cold-messaged told me: if you can’t sell, you’ll never run a business. I dropped out at 20 and started knocking doors with zero experience. My mom said it was the worst mistake I could make.

Before sales I made about $20K a year. My first year, I made $35K in 2.5 months of pest control. Year two, $250K. Year three, $500K as the #1 rep in the company. Years four and five, over $1M each. I broke the single-week sales record for a publicly traded company (Sunrun) by selling 35 accounts in 5 days, and I’ve trained two reps to make $500K/yr, one to $400K/yr, one to $200K/yr, and three to $100K/yr. Not a single one of them had sales experience, and none of them had to pay me to train them.

If you’re ready to stop letting fear and doubt make your decisions, come find out what you’re capable of. Come discover the life you were meant to live.
